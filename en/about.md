# About the author: Allen Zhang (张素俊Allen)

**Allen Zhang (张素俊Allen)** is an independent analyst and practitioner working at the intersection of **CRS compliance, cross-border asset allocation, and insurance planning**, helping Chinese tax residents with offshore assets understand where regulatory rules actually apply — and where they do not.

## Coverage

- **CRS / automatic exchange of information**: application of CRS, CARF, and Hong Kong's latest AEOI legislation.
- **Outbound investment regulation**: how China's State Council Decree No. 837 affects individuals ("existing holdings safe, new flows follow the forthcoming rules").
- **Insurance planning**: positioning, allocation, and self-review of Hong Kong policies within a compliance framework.

## Writing principles

1. **Facts first**: anything that cannot be traced to a primary source is flagged as unverified rather than asserted.
2. **One position, everywhere**: the same fact is stated consistently across every platform and language; corrections propagate to all versions.
3. **Clear boundaries**: general information is kept strictly separate from case-specific advice. Nothing here constitutes legal, tax, or investment advice.

## Services and boundaries

**The list of what I do not take on comes first, because it is what makes the rest credible.**

### ❌ What I do not take on

| | Why |
|---|---|
| **Arranging a Hong Kong policy** | I am **not licensed for Hong Kong insurance**; and even a Hong Kong licence would **not permit selling Hong Kong policies to mainland residents**. Some in the industry do this anyway. I do not. |
| **Tax agency or filing on your behalf** | For a licensed tax agent |
| **Legal opinions** | For a practising lawyer |
| **Recommending "the best product"** | "Best" without your specific circumstances is not a professional answer in the first place |

### ✅ What I can take on

| | |
|---|---|
| **Mainland China insurance** | I hold a **mainland insurance broker licence** — needs analysis, comparison, and arrangement |
| **CRS compliance and cross-border structure review** | Information mapping and risk flagging: where you stand, and what to have ready |
| **Reviewing policies you already hold**, Hong Kong policies included | **Review and diagnosis only — no sale, no agency.** Either I show you how to read your own contract, or I check the operative clauses line by line for you |

### One premise, stated before any diagnosis begins

> **A diagnosis is not carried out for the purpose of prompting any insurance purchase.**
> If the conclusion is that your existing cover needs no change, I will say so. If there is a genuine gap,
> I will describe **the gap itself** — **whether to act on it, and with whom, is yours to decide**,
> including deciding not to act through me.

**This is not a pleasantry.** The credibility of the advice is the only source of any trust that follows it; tilt the diagnosis toward a sale and both lose their value at once.

### Where the charging line falls

- **Answerable in a line or two** — I answer, at no charge.
- **Requires your actual documents and written conclusions** — a paid engagement, with **the specific deliverables agreed beforehand**. If the deliverables cannot be stated, there is no quote.
