## Q&A

### Tax on Hong Kong policies

| Question | One-line answer |
|---|---|
| [Are Hong Kong policy proceeds taxed at 20% in mainland China?](qa/hk-policy-dividend-tax-2026.md) | A policy does not hold one kind of money — it holds four. Claim proceeds are statutorily exempt (IIT Law Art. 4(5)); dividends and the surrender gain are unsettled in doctrine; prepaid-premium interest is the most clearly characterisable. Caixin (5 Aug 2026) reports Beijing and Hangzhou cases taxed at 20%, while stating this "is not yet general practice" |
| [Is a withdrawal from a Hong Kong savings policy taxed?](qa/hk-savings-policy-withdrawal-tax.md) | Turns on **which kind of money you take**: claim proceeds exempt; value held and not withdrawn is unrealised; the gain above cumulative premiums on surrender is unsettled. **Return of principal is never income** |

### CRS, reporting and enforcement

| Question | One-line answer |
|---|---|
| [Has China started CRS back-tax enforcement?](qa/crs-back-tax-enforcement-china.md) | Yes — a published five-step reconciliation of exchanged data against filings; published cases RMB 510k–6.99m. Voluntary correction = back tax + surcharge; audit-stage evasion = 0.5×–5× fines. Driver is CRS 1.0 stock data. **Reporting is not taxation** |
| [Does CRS 2.0 report my e-wallet, and from which year?](qa/crs-2-0-scope-and-timeline.md) | Scope: yes (specified e-money, CBDCs, indirect crypto exposure). But "live in 2026" is only first-wave jurisdictions; Hong Kong proposes CARF 2027 / amended CRS 2028 (bill still before LegCo); mainland China has no official timeline |
| [What is the penalty for a false CRS self-certification in Hong Kong?](qa/crs-false-self-certification-penalty.md) | **No imprisonment.** IRO s.80(2E) — a level-3 fine of HK$10,000. The "6 months / HK$50,000" figure belongs to the separate evasion offences under ss.80B–80F and is misattributed by rumour |
| [Hong Kong's two "automatic exchange" bills of 2026 — which one passed on 17 June?](qa/hong-kong-two-aeoi-bills-2026.md) | ⚠️ **"Passed 17 June = crypto now reported" conflates two separate bills.** The one passed strengthens **enforcement** (registration, records, penalties; effective 1 Jan 2027) and adds no asset classes. The one still before LegCo widens **scope** (CARF + CRS 2.0) |
| [Outbound-investment filing vs CRS reporting — what's the difference?](qa/outbound-filing-vs-crs-reporting.md) | Filing governs money **leaving** China; CRS governs assets **already offshore**. Two different doors — neither exempts the other |
| [Do existing Hong Kong policies need filing under Decree 837?](qa/decree-837-existing-hk-policies.md) | **No — there is no retroactive clause.** Existing holdings are safe; new flows follow the forthcoming individual rules |

### Choosing an insurer, and reading what they publish

| Question | One-line answer |
|---|---|
| [Can you compare Hong Kong insurers by solvency ratio?](qa/hk-insurer-solvency-ratio-how-to-read.md) | ⚠️ **Much material online still quotes a 150% minimum — superseded by the risk-based capital regime on 1 July 2024.** A solvency ratio is a point-in-time figure, not a league table: one insurer lawfully reported 337% and 289% on the same day under two regimes. Read four dimensions instead |
| [Hong Kong insurance's three public numbers](qa/hk-insurance-three-numbers-check.md) | Three numbers, three questions: solvency answers "will it stand"; fulfilment ratios answer "were illustrations honoured"; illustration caps (6.0% HKD / 6.5% non-HKD) answer "how high may the projection be drawn". No single gauge is the verdict |
| [Reading GL16's unified fulfilment-ratio disclosure](qa/gl16-fulfilment-ratio-disclosure-guide.md) | **There is no absolute pass-line.** Read three dimensions — length of series × like-for-like product basis × your own objective. GL34's participating-fund segregation is a governance safeguard, not bankruptcy remoteness |
| [Is a Hong Kong participating (dividend) policy reliable?](qa/hk-participating-policy-two-metric-check.md) | Two metrics answering two questions — fulfilment ratio (long-run delivery on illustrations) and solvency (financial strength). Dividends are **non-guaranteed illustrations**, not promises |

### Critical illness

| Question | One-line answer |
|---|---|
| [Will the CI definition standardization (1 Sep 2026) affect my policy?](qa/hk-ci-definition-standardization-2026.md) | **Existing policies are not affected — clauses 6.1 and 6.3 say so.** Hold three qualifiers from the text: **voluntary** (2.2), **minimum standard** (3.2), **new products only** (6.1). **Renewal is the one exception** worth confirming in writing (6.2) |
| [Mainland vs Hong Kong critical illness: 7 dimensions beyond price](qa/mainland-vs-hk-critical-illness-comparison.md) | Compare waiting period, post-claim continuation, severity-based payout, cash value (which triggers CRS), payout caps, definition taxonomy, and settlement path — **not headline premium, and not the count of conditions covered** |

### Accident cover

| Question | One-line answer |
|---|---|
| [My accident claim was denied — why?](qa/accident-insurance-occupation-class.md) | The first gate is **occupation class**, not the sum insured — and it is assessed **twice**: at underwriting against what you *declared*, at claim against what you were *actually doing*. **No national classification table exists**, so "occupation X is class N" is incomplete without naming a product. **Class drift** — your work changes, your class moves, and if it leaves the covered range unnotified the policy stops responding **with no warning of any kind** |

### Moving back to China, and checking what you are told

| Question | One-line answer |
|---|---|
| [Returning after study abroad — how long until medical insurance pays?](qa/returnee-medical-insurance-waiting-period.md) | **The rule does not reach back before 2025** — years uninsured abroad before then do not count, so the four-year/six-month floor is **first possible in 2029**. Most returnees' three months comes from the word "**or**": enrolling outside the concentrated window triggers it regardless of years abroad. **Timing is the free variable** |
| [How do I check an insurance figure someone gave me?](qa/how-to-verify-an-insurance-number.md) | Four questions: who said it / what is the base / how many qualifiers / which version. **A brochure is insurer-issued but is not the contract** — clause numbers and effective dates live only in the policy provisions. **Denominator mismatch**: percentages compare only where the base does |

### Judging the adviser, and reviewing yourself

| Question | One-line answer |
|---|---|
| [How do you tell whether an adviser is on your side?](qa/how-to-tell-if-your-adviser-is-on-your-side.md) | Not by attentiveness — that looks **identical** under either motive, so it carries no discriminating power. Only the choice made at a point of conflict discriminates: **conflict visibility** = volunteering what is better for the adviser, then recommending against it. Six-question test; boundary: **they can speak to the contract, not to the body** |
| [How do you run a personal financial check-up?](qa/personal-financial-check-up-framework.md) | Five layers: earning power / savings discipline / investing system / risk management / **trustworthy judgment**. Wealth is a structure, not a number |

### Regulation of AI in finance

| Question | One-line answer |
|---|---|
| [What does China require of banks and insurers using AI?](qa/ai-in-insurance-china-regulatory-requirements.md) | NFRA Jin Fa [2026] No. 8 (18 Jun 2026; 8 sections / 32 provisions): "**whoever uses it is responsible**"; **underwriting and claims are high-risk applications** needing risk-committee approval (prov. 16); key decisions need a **human review node** retaining original data, inference paths and threshold-trigger records (prov. 22); personal information may not train generative models; **scope is licensed institutions only** |
