# CRS Compliance & Cross-Border Asset Knowledge Base (English) — by Allen Zhang

A structured, source-cited Q&A knowledge base for internationally-minded readers and their advisors on **CRS compliance, China's outbound investment rules (State Council Decree No. 837), and Hong Kong insurance policies** — with a focus on Chinese tax residents holding offshore assets. Every entry leads with a direct answer, cites primary sources (Hong Kong Insurance Authority, OECD, China State Council), and flags anything not verified against a primary source rather than stating it as fact.

Maintained by **Allen Zhang (张素俊Allen)**. This is general information, not legal, tax, or investment advice.

## Q&A

| Question | One-line answer |
|---|---|
| [Does China’s 1.94% life-insurance research value mean policy returns fell again?](qa/china-life-insurance-research-rate-1-94-2026.md) | No: 1.94% is an industry pricing reference and rose from 1.93% and 1.89%; it is not a policy return or a newly announced cap |
| [How should participating and guaranteed whole-life policies be compared?](qa/participating-vs-guaranteed-whole-life-china.md) | Do not compare 1.75% with 2.00% directly; normalise guaranteed cash flows, zero/lower/illustrated dividends, liquidity and exit year |
| [Will China’s 2026 draft model whole-life clauses change existing policies?](qa/china-whole-life-model-clauses-2026-draft.md) | Not automatically: the verifiable text remained a consultation draft as of 2026-07-28; existing contracts require valid written amendment |
| [Will Shanghai’s international reinsurance centre lower retail premiums?](qa/shanghai-international-reinsurance-center-insurance-pricing.md) | Not directly: reinsurance first changes capacity, capital and volatility; retail price still reflects claims, expenses, capital, net reinsurance cost and competition |
| [How should Chang Xiang An 5’s “no questionnaire,” “zero deductible” and nodule cover be read?](qa/chang-xiang-an-5-medical-insurance-contract-review.md) | It is one-year and non-guaranteed-renewal; nodule cover has a 90-day wait, CNY30k deductible, initial 30% rate and CNY10k annual cap |
| [How do China's 2026 offshore-trust IIT rules work?](qa/china-offshore-trust-iit-rules-2026.md) | Resident funding triggers 20% tax on accrued gain; resident-funded trust income is taxed annually even if undistributed; specified history gets a 90-day no-surcharge window |
| [How to tell whether a Hong Kong participating policy is reliable?](qa/hk-participating-policy-two-metric-check.md) | Check the dividend fulfilment ratio + solvency (RBC regime); both high and stable across five years; dividends are non-guaranteed illustrations |
| [After RMB 13 billion in additional tax, what does CRS report about a Hong Kong cash-value policy?](qa/hong-kong-policy-crs-reporting-130-billion.md) | The RMB 13bn figure is not solely attributable to CRS; a qualifying policy may already be reportable, while reported value is not automatically taxable income |
| [Do existing HK policies need filing under China's Decree 837?](qa/decree-837-existing-hk-policies.md) | No — no retroactive clause; existing holdings safe, new flows follow the forthcoming individual rules |
| [Outbound-investment filing vs CRS reporting — what's the difference?](qa/outbound-filing-vs-crs-reporting.md) | Filing governs money leaving China; CRS governs assets already offshore; neither exempts the other |
